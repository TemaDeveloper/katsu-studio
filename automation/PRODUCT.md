# Katsu Studio
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User required Python/FastAPI backend and React frontend. Local macOS browser application.

## Users
The owner of Katsu The Printer, producing original English illustrated YouTube explainers.

## Product Purpose
Enter one topic and receive a complete narrated still-image video and its thumbnail, with the intermediate script, illustrations, sources, and timing available to inspect.

## Operating Context
OpenAI research, writing and image APIs; ElevenLabs voice API; local FFmpeg assembly. One-time provider setup, then automatic production. Eight-minute target is configurable. Recorded narration determines actual runtime.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Persistent projects, resumable work, image and narration edits, bounded estimated spending, cancellation, local MP4 export, and automatic thumbnail generation with separate regeneration. Thumbnails use original Katsu artwork and exact local lettering, exported as 3840 × 2160 JPEGs under 2 MB. No animated images or camera movement. No automatic publishing. Credentials remain on the backend in Keychain or environment variables. Paid APIs require separate accounts and billing.

## Brand Commitments
Katsu The Printer name and approved illustrated avatar. Expressive white oversized head, black stick limbs and curl, bold slightly uneven black outlines, predominantly white with yellow and coral accents.

## Evidence on Hand
Completed original episode and 120 illustrations in ../output. Approved avatar and banner in ../output/channel_branding. Real provider credentials are not configured; fixture tests must be identified as such.

## Product Principles
Automate routine production. Preserve successful work. Show real progress and actionable errors. Keep topic-to-video understandable. Do not misrepresent estimates or test fixtures as real provider results.
